Radiation dosimetry
CT dosimetry

The computed tomography dose index (CTDI) is the average phantom dose
for a single axial slice (one complete rotation without table motion), including
scatter, measured in Gy.
CTDI is measured with 16 cm and 32 cm phantoms. 16 cm will always have higher dose.

CTDIw (weighted) = 2/3 CTDIp + 1/3 CTDIc
CTDIp = peripheral
CTDIc = central

CTDIvol = CTDIw/pitch

The pitch is related to the table speed. A pitch <1 will overscan and result in increased
radiation exposure; conversely, a pitch >1 will skip areas but will decrease exposure.
CTDIvol is the same for all scans, independent of scan length.
CTDIvol should be less than reference values:
Adult head: 75 mGy (16 cm phantom)
Adult abdomen: 25 mGy (32 cm phantom)
Pediatric abdomen: 20 mGy (16 cm phantom)

DLP = CTDIvol * scan length = mGy*cm
