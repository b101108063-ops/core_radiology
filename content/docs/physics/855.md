Coherent scatter: No exchange of energy, no change in frequency,
and no contribution to patient dose. Contributes less than 5% of X-ray
interactions.

Compton scatter: Proportional to (electron density)/E. Scattered photons
go in all directions.

photons.

photon (keV)
