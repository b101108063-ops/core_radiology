Right middle lobe atelectasis

Right middle lobe atelectasis: Frontal chest radiograph shows an indistinct opacity in the right lung with
focal silhouetting of the right heart border (arrow). There is elevation of the right hemidiaphragm due
to volume loss. The lateral radiograph shows a wedge-shaped opacity (arrow) projecting over the midheart representing the collapsed right middle lobe.
Case courtesy Ritu R. Gill, MD, MPH, Brigham and Women’s Hospital.

•

The findings of right middle lobe atelectasis can be subtle on the frontal radiograph.
Silhouetting of the right heart border by the collapsed medial segment of the middle
lobe may be the only clue. The lateral radiograph shows a wedge-shaped opacity
anteriorly.
