## Text Content

### 纖維化縱膈炎 / Fibrosing Mediastinitis — WHO Group 5

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| **Pathology** | Progressive fibrous tissue proliferation within the mediastinum |
| **Most common causes** | **Histoplasmosis** and **tuberculosis** |
| **Complications** | Pulmonary vein encasement → PVH; pulmonary artery encasement → precapillary PH |
| **Imaging** | Increased mediastinal soft tissue; **calcified lymph nodes** from prior granulomatous infection |

---

### 肺栓塞 / Pulmonary Embolism (PE)

#### 臨床診斷 / Clinical Diagnosis

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| **Symptoms** | Dyspnea, tachycardia, pleuritic chest pain (common and nonspecific) |
| **Risk factors** | Immobilization, malignancy, catheters, obesity, oral contraceptives, thrombophilia |
| **~25%** | No identifiable risk factor |
| **Diagnostic tools** | **Wells score** for clinical probability; **D-dimer** (high NPV but many false positives in inpatients) |

---

### 影像學表現 / CT Pulmonary Angiography (CTPA)

| Finding | Description |
|---|---|
| **Typical PE appearance** | **Central intraluminal filling defect** in pulmonary artery |
| **Emboli location** | Tend to lodge at vessel bifurcations |
| **Chronic PE sign** | **Eccentric, circumferential** filling defect |
| **Associated findings** | Wedge-shaped consolidation, pleural effusion, linear subsegmental atelectasis (nonspecific) |

### 臨床要點 / Clinical Key Points
> - **Fibrosing mediastinitis** from histoplasmosis/TB can cause both postcapillary (vein) and precapillary (artery) pulmonary hypertension
> - **CTPA** is the standard imaging for PE — look for filling defects at arterial bifurcations
> - **Pleural effusions and consolidation** are seen equally in patients with and without PE — not diagnostic
> - **D-dimer** has limited utility in hospitalized patients due to false positives from comorbid conditions
