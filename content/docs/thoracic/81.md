## Text Content

### Castleman 病 / Castleman Disease

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| **Also called** | Angiofollicular lymph node hyperplasia |
| **Etiology** | Unknown; highly vascular lymph node enlargement |
| **Localized** | Children/young adults; surgical resection usually curative |
| **Multicentric** | Older patients or AIDS; systemic illness (fever, anemia); often progresses to lymphoma; treated with chemotherapy |
| **Key imaging** | **Avidly enhancing adenopathy** |

---

### 前縱膈腫塊 — 心前區（下前縱膈）/ Anterior Mediastinal Mass — Precardiac

| Entity | Key Features |
|---|---|
| **Epicardial fat pad** | May silhouette cardiac border; can simulate cardiomegaly on CXR |
| **Pericardial cyst** | Benign; most common at **right cardiophrenic angle**; cystic lesion abutting pericardium; changes shape between studies |
| **Morgagni hernia** | Through foramen of Morgagni; contains **omental fat ± bowel**; usually right-sided; omental vessels on CT trace to upper abdomen |

---

### 中縱膈腫塊 / Middle Mediastinal Mass

| Entity | Key Features |
|---|---|
| **Lymphadenopathy** | Most common cause |
| **Ascending aortic / arch aneurysm** | May simulate mediastinal mass on CXR |

### 臨床要點 / Clinical Key Points
> - **Pericardial cyst** at the right cardiophrenic angle is a classic benign mediastinal lesion — look for fluid attenuation on CT
> - **Morgagni hernia** with bowel gas on CXR/CT is diagnostic — look for omental vessels tracing to the abdomen
> - **Castleman disease** with avidly enhancing nodes in a young person = think localized (curable) vs. multicentric (systemic)
> - Always check for **continuity with the thyroid** in any superior mediastinal mass — goiter is the most common thyroid lesion extending into the mediastinum
