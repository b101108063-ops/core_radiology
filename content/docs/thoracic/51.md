## Text Content

### 肺癌分期案例 / Lung Cancer Staging — Case Examples

---

### 案例一：Stage IIIA

**T2N2M0 = Stage IIIA**

| Finding | Stage Descriptor |
|---|---|
| Right upper lobe peripheral mass, abuts pleura | **T2** (size >3 cm + visceral pleural invasion) |
| Ipsilateral right paratracheal adenopathy | **N2** (ipsilateral mediastinal nodes) |
| No distant metastasis | **M0** |

**Overall Stage: IIIA** — potentially **resectable**

---

### 案例二：Stage IV (M1a — Contralateral Nodule)

**T2M1a = Stage IV**

| Finding | Stage Descriptor |
|---|---|
| Right upper lobe spiculated mass | **T2** (>3–5 cm) |
| Small contralateral lung nodule (proven malignant) | **M1a** (contralateral lung) |

**Overall Stage: IV** — not surgical

---

### 案例三：Stage IV (M1b — Adrenal Metastases)

**T2N1M1b = Stage IV**

| Finding | Stage Descriptor |
|---|---|
| Left upper lobe mass + ipsilateral hilar adenopathy | **T2** + **N1** |
| Bilateral adrenal masses (proven metastases) | **M1b** (distant) |

**Overall Stage: IV** — systemic therapy; surgery not indicated

---

### 分期要點 / Staging Key Points

| Scenario | Resectability |
|---|---|
| **T2, N0–1, M0** (Stage I–II) | Surgical |
| **N2** (IIIA) | Multimodality; selected cases may be surgical |
| **N3** (IIIB) | **Unresectable** |
| **M1** (Stage IV) | **Not surgical** (except solitary adrenal/brain in select cases) |

### 臨床要點 / Clinical Key Points
> - **Stage IIIA** with limited N2 disease may be resectable — depends on number and station of involved nodes
> - **Any M1 disease** (pleural effusion, contralateral nodule, distant mets) = Stage IV — curative surgery not possible
> - Always look for **contralateral lung nodules** and **adrenal masses** in lung cancer staging CT
> - **Adrenal masses** in lung cancer patients are metastases until proven otherwise — most are benign adrenal adenomas
