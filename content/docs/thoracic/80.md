## Text Content

### 甲狀腺病變 / Thyroid Lesions

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| **Types** | Benign/malignant thyroid masses extending into mediastinum; goiter, thyroid neoplasm, thyroiditis |
| **Key diagnostic clue** | **Continuity superiorly with the thyroid gland** |
| **Imaging** | Heterogeneous mass with coarse calcifications replacing the thyroid |
| **Embryology** | Extends posteriorly via fascial plane created by the inferior thyroidal artery |

---

### 淋巴瘤 / Lymphoma

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| **Types** | Hodgkin disease and non-Hodgkin lymphoma |
| **Hodgkin disease** | Commonly involves thorax; superior mediastinal nodes (prevascular, AP window, paratracheal) |
| **Non-Hodgkin lymphoma** | Diverse group; less commonly involves thorax than Hodgkin |
| **Calcification** | Rare in untreated lymphoma |

---

### 非淋巴瘤性淋巴結病 / Non-Lymphomatous Lymphadenopathy

| Finding | Associated Conditions |
|---|---|
| **Eggshell calcification** | Silicosis, CWP; also sarcoidosis |
| **Dense calcification** | Sarcoidosis; prior granulomatous disease |
| **Low attenuation nodes** | Active tuberculosis, fungal infection, lymphoma, metastases |
| **Avid enhancement** | **Castleman disease**, sarcoidosis, TB, vascular metastases (renal cell, thyroid, lung, sarcoma, melanoma) |

### 臨床要點 / Clinical Key Points
> - **Eggshell calcification** of lymph nodes in a patient with occupational exposure = silicosis or CWP
> - **Avidly enhancing adenopathy** = think Castleman disease, sarcoidosis, or vascular metastases (thyroid, renal cell)
> - **Low-attenuation lymph nodes** in a patient from an endemic area = tuberculosis
> - Lymphoma rarely calcifies before treatment — if you see calcification, consider prior therapy or alternative diagnosis
