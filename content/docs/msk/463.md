             •   The distal suprascapular nerve provides motor innervation to the infraspinatus muscle
                 only. Entrapment of the distal suprascapular nerve at the spinoglenoid notch will
                 cause isolated atrophy of the infraspinatus.

Parsonage–Turner
             •   Parsonage–Turner syndrome is idiopathic brachial neuropathy, which may cause
                 rotator cuff atrophy. It is a diagnosis of exclusion and a structural cause for atrophy
                 (e.g., a mass or cyst compressing a nerve) must be ruled out.
