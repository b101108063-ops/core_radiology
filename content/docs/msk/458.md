             •   The glenoid labrum is a fibrocartilaginous ring-like structure that rests on the glenoid
                 hyaline cartilage and surrounds the glenoid. The labrum increases the surface area
                 and depth of the glenoid cavity. It also provides a suction effect, adhering the humeral
                 head to the glenoid with motion.

             •   A sublabral foramen (seen in 10% of patients) is a normal variant where the
