Diastematomyelia




                   Diastematomyelia: Coronal T2-weighted MRI (left image) shows CSF between the two hemicords
                   (arrows) and a thoracolumbar dextroscoliosis. Axial T2-weighted MRI (right image) shows two
                   hemicords (arrows) within a single CSF space.
              •    Diastematomyelia is a congenitally split spinal cord, which may be a cause of scoliosis.
