•   In the skull base, fibrous dysplasia is typically expansile and can look highly unusual
    on MRI. The primary differential of an expansile skull base lesion is Paget disease, but
    the age of the patient is the key: fibrous dysplasia occurs in children and young adults,
    while Paget occurs in older adults.
