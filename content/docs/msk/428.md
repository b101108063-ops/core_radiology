              •   After ACL reconstruction, the ACL graft should run parallel to the intercondylar notch,

Posterior cruciate ligament (PCL)
              •   The posterior cruciate ligament (PCL) is a stronger, thicker ligament than the ACL. It
                  also arises from the femoral intercondylar notch (more anteriorly than the ACL), but
                  inserts on the posterior tibial plateau.
                      The PCL is normally low signal on all sequences.
                      The PCL is much less commonly injured than the ACL.
PCL tear
              •   In contrast to an ACL tear, a complete disruption of the PCL fibers is uncommon.
              •   The typical MRI appearance of PCL injury is increased laxity of the PCL, with or
                  without abnormal high signal on T2-weighted images.
