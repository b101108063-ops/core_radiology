## 總覽

本章為**介入放射學（Interventional Radiology, IR）**之核心診療指南，完整收錄血管造影診斷、動脈栓塞處置、解剖變異識別、消化道與泌尿膽道非血管介入，以及周邊血管疾病之腔內治療技術。內容嚴謹結合血管解剖標記、介入器材規格（導絲、導管與血管鞘）、適應症、禁忌症與致命併發症之緊急避險處置（如空氣栓塞與完全性房室傳導阻滯）。全章劃分為八大核心主題：總覽、血管造影基礎、胸部、腹盆腔、壓迫/門脈、膽道與非血管、下肢，以及上肢與文獻。每一小節均搭配高解析度影像，並提供 2–4 句具高度臨床實務價值的雙語解析與關鍵數據。

## 血管造影基礎

### 介入放射學導論與目錄概觀
![介入放射學導論與目錄概觀](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-703.png)

**中文：** 本章節完整規劃血管造影解剖、穿刺路徑與導管導絲選擇，為血管與非血管介入處置提供系統化藍圖。臨床評估需精準掌握血管解剖變異與潛在併發症，以確保患者處置安全與診療品質。全章涵蓋動脈栓塞、腔內支架、膽道引流與經皮尿路處置等核心內容。

**English:** This introductory section outlines the comprehensive blueprint for interventional radiology procedures, covering vascular diagnostics, arterial embolization, and non-vascular interventions. Clinical practice mandates a thorough understanding of vascular anatomy, anatomical variants, and potential procedural complications. Systematic review of access routes, catheter selection, and device specifications ensures optimal patient safety and procedural success.

### 血管造影影像判讀與相別
![血管造影影像判讀與相別](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-704.png)

**中文：** 血管造影影像判讀需系統化評估四大相別：早期動脈相、晚期動脈相、實質相（Parenchymal phase）與靜脈相（Venous phase）。臨床可運用 <span style="color:#60a5fa">VIA 口訣</span>（Vessels 血管性狀, Interventions 介入處置物, Anything else 異常發現）完整審視造影種類、導管位置與血管輪廓。若動脈相提前出現靜脈顯影，提示存在 <span style="color:#f87171">動靜脈分流（AV shunt）</span>；同時需檢視有無活性對比劑外溢、夾層、狹窄或術後留置的支架、濾鏡與引流管。

**English:** Interpretation of angiographic studies requires a systematic approach evaluating four distinct opacification phases: early arterial, late arterial, parenchymal, and venous phases. The mnemonic VIA (vessels, interventions, and anything else) provides a reliable framework to assess catheter position, vessel contours, and phase timing. Early venous opacification during the arterial phase strongly suggests an arteriovenous shunt or abnormal neovascularity. Comprehensive review must also identify active contrast extravasation, arterial dissection, stenosis, and previously placed vascular stents, filters, or surgical drains.

### 股動脈穿刺技術與相關併發症
![股動脈穿刺技術與相關併發症](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-705.png)

**中文：** 股動脈穿刺建議於 <span style="color:#22d3ee">股骨頭中下內側緣（Inferomedial margin of femoral head）</span> 進針，此處骨骼可提供加壓止血之堅硬支撐。逆行穿刺（至主動脈）針頭向頭側傾斜 <span style="color:#60a5fa">45 度</span>，順行穿刺（至下肢）向腳側傾斜 45 度。高位穿刺（高於腹股溝韌帶/腹壁下動脈）極易引發高危險的 <span style="color:#f87171">腹膜後血腫（Retroperitoneal hematoma）</span>；過低穿刺則易貫穿股靜脈形成 <span style="color:#f87171">動靜脈廔管（AVF）</span>。直徑 > 1 cm 的假性動脈瘤（Pseudoaneurysm）首選 <span style="color:#60a5fa">超音波導引凝血酶注射（US-guided thrombin injection）</span> 處置。

**English:** The optimal location for common femoral artery access is over the inferomedial margin of the femoral head, which provides a rigid bony surface for effective post-procedural compression. Retrograde arterial access requires a 45-degree cranial needle angle toward the aorta, whereas anterograde access utilizes a 45-degree caudal angle toward the leg. High arterial punctures above the pelvic brim significantly increase the risk of life-threatening retroperitoneal hematoma. Low punctures below the femoral head carry a higher risk of arteriovenous fistula formation and pseudoaneurysm, with pseudoaneurysms larger than 1 cm treated via ultrasound-guided thrombin injection.

### 通用注意事項：空氣栓塞、注射速率、PTA 與支架
![通用注意事項：空氣栓塞、注射速率、PTA 與支架](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-706.png)

**中文：** 血管介入最危險步驟為導管穿入撕開式外殼時，若懷疑發生 <span style="color:#f87171">空氣栓塞（Air embolism）</span>，應立即將患者置於 <span style="color:#60a5fa">左側臥位（Left-lateral decubitus）</span> 並給予 100% 氧氣。血管造影注射速率粗略依血管毫米直徑決定（如主動脈弓 20 mL/s 共 30 mL，腸系膜動脈 5 mL/s 共 25 mL）。氣球血管成形術（PTA）氣球直徑應比血管大 <span style="color:#60a5fa">10–20%</span> 且必須全程使用 <span style="color:#fb923c">肝素（Heparin）</span> 抗凝。支架選擇上，氣球擴張式支架徑向力高但被壓扁後無法復原，故受外壓部位（如關節、亨特氏管或壓迫症候群）應選擇 <span style="color:#60a5fa">自擴式支架（Self-expandable stent）</span>；支架直徑選用規則為動脈過大 <span style="color:#34d399">10%</span>、靜脈過大 <span style="color:#34d399">20%</span>。

**English:** Air embolism during venous access catheter insertion into a peel-away sheath requires immediate placement of the patient in the left-lateral decubitus position and administration of 100% oxygen. Contrast injection rates follow vessel diameter guidelines, such as 20 mL/s for 30 mL total in the aortic arch and 5 mL/s for 25 mL in mesenteric arteries. Percutaneous transluminal angioplasty requires balloons sized 10% to 20% larger than the vessel lumen and mandatory heparin anticoagulation to minimize acute thrombosis. Balloon-expandable stents offer superior radial force but permanently deform if crushed, making self-expandable stents mandatory for anatomical sites subject to external compression; stents are oversized by 10% in arteries and 20% in veins.

### 栓塞原理與材料、併發症與導管規格
![栓塞原理與材料、併發症與導管規格](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-707.png)

**中文：** 栓塞材料分為永久性（微彈簧圈 Coils、PVA 顆粒、組織膠 Cyanoacrylate、硬化劑 STS）與暫時性（<span style="color:#60a5fa">明膠海綿 Gelfoam，維持 2–6 週</span>，術後 CT 氣泡為正常現象）。微彈簧圈栓塞應採取 <span style="color:#60a5fa">「前後門（Distal then proximal）」</span> 順序以防側支逆流出血，但會永久犧牲遠端再進入路徑。處置後併發症包含 <span style="color:#f87171">栓塞後症候群（Post-embolization syndrome）</span>（發燒、疼痛、噁心，以 NSAIDs 與輸液控制）及非目標栓塞。導管尺寸以 <span style="color:#60a5fa">French（1 Fr = 0.33 mm）</span> 標示外徑，血管鞘標示值則為內徑；高流量沖洗導管（如 Pigtail）具多側孔，超選擇性導管（如 C2、SOS、Berenstein）則為單端孔。

**English:** Embolic materials encompass permanent agents such as metallic coils, PVA particles, cyanoacrylate glue, and STS sclerosant, alongside temporary agents like absorbable gelatin sponge lasting 2 to 6 weeks. Embolic coil deployment requires a distal-then-proximal placement technique to exclude retrograde collateral blood flow while accepting the permanent loss of distal vessel re-access. Post-embolization syndrome presents with fever, pain, cramping, and nausea within 24 hours of infarction and is managed supportively with NSAIDs, opioids, and intravenous fluids. Catheters are sized in French units corresponding to outer diameter (1 Fr = 0.33 mm), whereas sheaths are designated by inner diameter; high-flow flush catheters utilize multiple sideholes, while selective catheters feature single terminal orifices.

### 導絲規格分類與巨細胞動脈炎造影
![導絲規格分類與巨細胞動脈炎造影](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-708.png)

**中文：** 介入導絲標準外徑為 <span style="color:#60a5fa">0.035 吋</span>，微導絲為 <span style="color:#60a5fa">0.018 吋</span>。導絲類型包含標準軟頭/J 型導絲（Bentson, Rosen）、親水性導絲（Glidewire, Roadrunner，用於穿越狹窄或初次置管）與高剛性導絲（Amplatz superstiff，用於擴張器或引流管過線置入）。胸部血管疾病中，<span style="color:#f87171">巨細胞動脈炎（Giant Cell Arteritis, GCA）</span> 好發於 > 50 歲患者，主要累及中型上肢動脈（鎖骨下動脈、腋動脈、肱動脈），造影呈長段平滑狹窄與阻塞，主動脈極少受累，確診依賴 <span style="color:#22d3ee">顳動脈切片（Temporal artery biopsy）</span> 並以類固醇治療。

**English:** Interventional wires are categorized by diameter into standard 0.035-inch wires and 0.018-inch microwires, featuring specialized floppy-tip, hydrophilic, or superstiff structural designs. Hydrophilic wires such as Glidewires facilitate crossing tight vessel stenoses, while superstiff Amplatz wires provide necessary support for track dilation and sheath insertion. Giant cell arteritis primarily affects patients over 50 years of age, characteristically involving medium-sized upper extremity vessels including subclavian, axillary, and brachial arteries with long smooth stenoses. Unlike Takayasu arteritis, the thoracic aorta is rarely affected in giant cell arteritis, and definitive diagnosis requires temporal artery biopsy followed by systemic corticosteroid therapy.

## 胸部

### 胸主動脈與上腔靜脈發育變異
![胸主動脈與上腔靜脈發育變異](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-709.png)

**中文：** 胸主動脈病變參見心血管影像章節。上腔靜脈（SVC）先天發育異常包括 <span style="color:#22d3ee">持久性左側上腔靜脈（Persistent left SVC）</span>，此為胚胎期左前基底靜脈未退化所致（發生率約 0.3–0.5%），左側 SVC 下行匯入 <span style="color:#22d3ee">擴張的冠狀靜脈竇（Coronary sinus）</span> 回流至右心房。其他胸腔大靜脈變異尚包含上腔靜脈重複（Duplicated SVC）與下腔靜脈發育異常，臨床進行中央靜脈置管或造影時需精準識別，避免將異位靜脈導管誤判為血管穿孔或縱隔腔誤置。

**English:** Congenital anomalies of the thoracic venous system include persistent left-sided superior vena cava, resulting from failure of involution of the embryonic left anterior cardinal vein in 0.3% to 0.5% of the population. A persistent left superior vena cava typically courses adjacent to the left mediastinum and drains directly into an enlarged coronary sinus. Additional venous malformations include duplicated superior vena cava and anomalous inferior vena cava pathways. Precise recognition of these thoracic venous variants during central venous catheterization or fluoroscopy prevents misinterpretation of venous placement as arterial perforation or mediastinal extravasation.

### 肺動脈造影技術與心導管併發症防範
![肺動脈造影技術與心導管併發症防範](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-710.png)

**中文：** 進行肺動脈造影前，必須仔細核對患者心電圖。若患者已知存在 <span style="color:#f87171">左束支傳導阻滯（LBBB）</span>，導管通過右心室時誘發的暫時性右束支傳導阻滯（RBBB）將導致致命的 <span style="color:#f87171">完全性房室傳導阻滯（Complete heart block）</span>，此類患者必須預先放置 <span style="color:#fb923c">臨時心臟起搏器（Temporary pacer）</span>。正常肺動脈收縮壓為 15–30 mmHg、舒張壓 5–15 mmHg、平均壓約 <span style="color:#34d399">15 mmHg</span>；當肺動脈壓力極高（平均壓 > 40 mmHg）時，造影併發症風險激增，應減少對比劑用量或行選擇性注射。

**English:** Prior to performing pulmonary artery angiography, evaluating a baseline electrocardiogram is mandatory to screen for pre-existing left bundle branch block. Catheter traversal through the right ventricle can induce transient right bundle branch block, which in the presence of underlying LBBB precipitates fatal complete heart block. Patients with baseline LBBB must have a temporary cardiac pacemaker placed prior to catheter manipulation across the right ventricle. Normal pulmonary artery pressures are 15-30 mmHg systolic, 5-15 mmHg diastolic, and a mean of 15 mmHg, with severe pulmonary hypertension requiring reduced contrast volume to avoid hemodynamic collapse.

### 支氣管動脈造影與大咯血栓塞術
![支氣管動脈造影與大咯血栓塞術](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-711.png)

**中文：** 支氣管動脈栓塞術（BAE）為處置大咯血（Hemoptysis）的首選微創止血治療。支氣管動脈通常發自 T5–T6 高度的胸降主動脈。造影過程中最嚴重的危險為誤栓發自支氣管動脈或肋間支氣管幹的 <span style="color:#22d3ee">脊髓前動脈（Artery of Adamkiewicz）</span>，其在造影下呈典型 <span style="color:#22d3ee">髮夾彎（Hairpin loop）</span> 特徵，誤栓將引發不可逆的 <span style="color:#f87171">脊髓缺血與截癱（Paraplegia）</span>。栓塞可選用 PVA 顆粒或微球，處置前需仔細確認無脊髓動脈顯影。

**English:** Bronchial artery embolization represents the primary endovascular intervention for managing life-threatening hemoptysis, with culprit bronchial arteries typically originating from the descending thoracic aorta at T5 to T6. Angiographic assessment before embolization requires meticulous exclusion of the anterior spinal artery (Artery of Adamkiewicz), which displays a characteristic hairpin loop appearance. Unintended non-target embolization of the anterior spinal artery results in spinal cord infarction and permanent paraplegia. Embolization is typically performed using polyvinyl alcohol particles or microspheres once safe catheter positioning distal to spinal branches is fluoroscopically verified.

## 腹盆腔

### 腹盆腔血管解剖與骨骼標記
![腹盆腔血管解剖與骨骼標記](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-712.png)

**中文：** 腹主動脈主要分支的骨骼解剖發源高度為：<span style="color:#22d3ee">腹腔動脈幹（Celiac artery）發自 T12 椎體</span>；<span style="color:#22d3ee">腸系膜上動脈（SMA）發自 T12–L1 椎間盤</span>；雙側 <span style="color:#22d3ee">腎動脈發自 L1–L2 椎間盤</span>；腸系膜下動脈（IMA）發自 L2–L3 椎間盤高度。約 75% 患者具經典腹腔幹三分支（左胃動脈、肝總動脈、脾動脈）。左胃動脈為食道 Mallory-Weiss 裂傷出血源；左胃網膜動脈發自脾動脈，並於胃大彎與發自胃十二指腸動脈（GDA）的右胃網膜動脈形成吻合。

**English:** The main visceral branches of the abdominal aorta correlate with specific vertebral osseous landmarks: the celiac artery arises at T12, the superior mesenteric artery at T12-L1, the renal arteries at L1-L2, and the inferior mesenteric artery at L2-L3. Approximately 75% of individuals demonstrate classic celiac axis anatomy with three main branches: left gastric, common hepatic, and splenic arteries. The left gastric artery represents a common source of upper gastrointestinal bleeding in esophageal Mallory-Weiss tears. The left gastroepiploic artery arises from the splenic artery and forms a robust anastomotic network along the greater gastric curvature with the right gastroepiploic artery originating from the gastroduodenal artery.

### 腹腔動脈幹選擇性造影解剖
![腹腔動脈幹選擇性造影解剖](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-713.png)

**中文：** 選擇性腹腔動脈幹數位減影造影（DSA）早期動脈相可清楚顯影三大主幹分支：<span style="color:#22d3ee">左胃動脈（Left gastric）</span>、<span style="color:#22d3ee">脾動脈（Splenic）</span> 與 <span style="color:#22d3ee">肝總動脈（Common hepatic）</span>。肝總動脈發出向下行走的胃十二指腸動脈（GDA）後改稱為肝固有動脈（PHA），進一步分出左肝動脈與右肝動脈（膽囊動脈多發自右肝動脈）。精準識別 GDA 為腹腔幹與 SMA 側支交通的重要關鍵。

**English:** Selective digital subtraction angiography of the celiac trunk in the early arterial phase outlines its three primary branches: the left gastric, splenic, and common hepatic arteries. The common hepatic artery gives off the gastroduodenal artery inferiorly before continuing as the proper hepatic artery, which divides into right and left hepatic branches. The cystic artery typically arises from the right hepatic artery, while the gastroduodenal artery serves as the essential collateral connection between the celiac axis and the superior mesenteric artery. Accurate catheterization of these branches is vital for localized oncologic and hemorrhagic endovascular therapies.

### 肝動脈典型解剖與替代右肝動脈
![肝動脈典型解剖與替代右肝動脈](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-714.png)

**中文：** 經典解剖中（占 75%），肝固有動脈為肝臟主要供血來源。最常見的肝動脈變異為 <span style="color:#60a5fa">替代右肝動脈（Replaced Right Hepatic Artery, RRHA，占 10–18%）</span>，此異位右肝動脈直接發自 <span style="color:#22d3ee">腸系膜上動脈（SMA）</span>。在進行胰十二指腸切除術（Whipple procedure）或肝臟腫瘤栓塞（TACE）前，必須於 SMA 造影中精準辨識 RRHA，避免手術誤切引發右肝缺血壞死或膽道併發症。

**English:** The proper hepatic artery supplies the liver in 75% of individuals, representing the continuation of the common hepatic artery after giving off the gastroduodenal artery. The most common hepatic arterial variant is a replaced right hepatic artery, present in 10% to 18% of patients, where the right hepatic artery arises directly from the superior mesenteric artery. A replaced right hepatic artery travels posterior to the portal vein and head of the pancreas, requiring mandatory pre-operative identification before pancreaticoduodenectomy or transarterial chemoembolization to prevent hepatic infarction.

### 替代左肝動脈與副肝動脈變異
![替代左肝動脈與副肝動脈變異](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-715.png)

**中文：** <span style="color:#60a5fa">替代左肝動脈（Replaced Left Hepatic Artery, RLHA）占 11–12%</span>，此變異血管係直接發自 <span style="color:#22d3ee">左胃動脈（Left gastric artery）</span>，並經由靜脈韌帶裂進入左肝。若進行胃部手術或因左胃動脈出血行栓塞治療時，需特別注意辨識 RLHA，避免誤栓引發 <span style="color:#f87171">左肝缺血性壞死</span>。副左肝動脈（Accessory left hepatic artery）則為左肝固有動脈外額外發自左胃動脈的分支。

**English:** A replaced left hepatic artery is observed in 11% to 12% of individuals, originating from the left gastric artery and traversing the fissure for the ligamentum venosum into the liver. During gastric surgery or left gastric artery embolization for gastrointestinal hemorrhage, identifying an RLHA is crucial to avoid inadvertent hepatic parenchyma devascularization. An accessory left hepatic artery represents a supplementary left hepatic vessel arising from the left gastric artery while the normal proper hepatic arterial left branch remains intact.

### 內髂動脈分支與腸系膜下動脈解剖
![內髂動脈分支與腸系膜下動脈解剖](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-716.png)

**中文：** 內髂動脈（Internal iliac artery）分為前枝與後枝。後枝分支包含 <span style="color:#22d3ee">腸腰動脈、外側骶骨動脈與臀上動脈（Superior gluteal）</span>；前枝分支包含 <span style="color:#22d3ee">臀下動脈、閉孔動脈、陰部內動脈（Internal pudendal）、子宮動脈、直腸中動脈與膀胱上動脈</span>。腸系膜下動脈（IMA）則發出左結腸動脈、乙狀結腸動脈並延續為直腸上動脈，為降結腸與直腸提供供血與豐富盆腔側支。

**English:** The internal iliac artery divides into posterior and anterior divisions to supply the pelvic visceral organs and musculoskeletal walls. The posterior division yields the iliolumbar, lateral sacral, and superior gluteal arteries, whereas the anterior division gives rise to the inferior gluteal, obturator, internal pudendal, uterine, middle rectal, and superior vesical arteries. The inferior mesenteric artery branches into the left colic and sigmoid arteries before terminating as the superior rectal artery, providing essential visceral blood supply and rich pelvic collateral connections.

### 腹腔主要血管吻合途徑與 Buhler 弧
![腹腔主要血管吻合途徑與 Buhler 弧](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-717.png)

**中文：** 腹腔動脈幹與 SMA 之間最主要的側支吻合為 <span style="color:#22d3ee">胰十二指腸動脈弓（Pancreaticoduodenal cascade）</span>，由 GDA 的胰十二指腸上動脈與 SMA 的胰十二指腸下動脈相連。罕見的胚胎殘留血管 <span style="color:#60a5fa">Buhler 弧（Arc of Buhler）</span> 為直接連接腹腔幹主幹與 SMA 主幹的動脈通道，當腹腔幹發生慢性狹窄或阻塞時，Buhler 弧或胰十二指腸動脈弓會顯著擴張以維持肝脾器官灌注。

**English:** The primary anastomotic pathway between the celiac axis and the superior mesenteric artery is the pancreaticoduodenal arterial cascade, formed by superior branches from the GDA and inferior branches from the SMA. A rare congenital vascular remnant termed the Arc of Buhler represents a direct arterial connection between the main trunks of the celiac axis and SMA. In the setting of chronic celiac axis stenosis or occlusion, the Arc of Buhler and pancreaticoduodenal pathways undergo compensatory hypertrophy to maintain hepatic and visceral perfusion.

### SMA 與 IMA 吻合網：Drummond 邊緣動脈與 Riolan 弧
![SMA 與 IMA 吻合網：Drummond 邊緣動脈與 Riolan 弧](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-718.png)

**中文：** SMA 與 IMA 之間最重要的側支吻合為 <span style="color:#22d3ee">Drummond 邊緣動脈（Marginal artery of Drummond）</span>，其位於結腸周邊腸系膜內，由迴結腸、右結腸、中結腸與左結腸動脈分支組成。位置較靠中央的吻合血管為 <span style="color:#60a5fa">Riolan 弧（Arc of Riolan，又稱迂曲腸系膜動脈）</span>，連接中結腸動脈與左結腸動脈。當 SMA 或 IMA 發生慢性阻塞時，這些吻合支會顯著擴張以預防結腸缺血。

**English:** The marginal artery of Drummond forms the primary collateral network between the superior and inferior mesenteric arteries, situated in the peripheral mesentery adjacent to the colon wall. This marginal pathway integrates terminal branches of the ileocolic, right colic, middle colic, and left colic arteries into a continuous conduit. The Arc of Riolan, or meandering mesenteric artery, represents a more central anastomotic channel directly connecting the middle colic branch of the SMA to the left colic branch of the IMA during chronic arterial obstruction.

### 脾動脈瘤、內臟動脈瘤與急性腸系膜缺血
![脾動脈瘤、內臟動脈瘤與急性腸系膜缺血](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-719.png)

**中文：** 脾動脈瘤為最常見內臟動脈瘤，多見於多產婦與門脈高壓患者，懷孕期破裂風險極高。治療門檻為 <span style="color:#60a5fa">直徑 > 2.5 cm</span>、有症狀或準備懷孕，採 <span style="color:#60a5fa">遠端再近端（Sandwich）彈簧圈栓塞</span>。肝動脈瘤栓塞應選擇右肝動脈膽囊動脈開口遠端，避免膽囊缺血。急性腸系膜缺血多由 SMA 遠端栓塞引起；非阻塞性腸系膜缺血（NOMI）死亡率高達 70–100%，需直接動脈灌注血管擴張劑 <span style="color:#fb923c">Papaverine（60 mg 衝擊量，後 30–60 mg/h）</span> 治療。

**English:** Splenic artery aneurysms represent the most common visceral artery aneurysms, occurring predominantly in multiparous women and patients with portal hypertension, with a high rupture risk during pregnancy. Treatment is indicated for symptomatic aneurysms, size exceeding 2.5 cm, or prior to anticipated pregnancy, utilizing a distal-then-proximal coil embolization technique. Non-occlusive mesenteric ischemia carries a 70% to 100% mortality rate and is treated with direct intra-arterial infusion of papaverine at a bolus of 60 mg followed by 30 to 60 mg/h to relieve severe mesenteric vasospasm.

### 慢性腸系膜缺血與消化道出血介入
![慢性腸系膜缺血與消化道出血介入](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-720.png)

**中文：** 因腸系膜側支循環極為豐富，慢性腸系膜缺血通常需腹腔幹、SMA 與 IMA 中 <span style="color:#f87171">至少 2 支發生嚴重病變</span> 方會產生餐後腹痛症狀，治療採氣球擴張與支架置放。下消化道出血時，DSA 診斷門檻為出血速率 0.5–1.0 mL/min，<span style="color:#60a5fa">核醫標記紅血球掃描可感測 0.2–0.4 mL/min</span>，CTA 則可感測 0.35 mL/min。動脈灌注 <span style="color:#fb923c">Vasopressin（0.2–0.4 units/min）</span> 可控制下消化道出血，但使用上限為 <span style="color:#f87171">24 小時</span>（過後會產生耐藥性 Tachyphylaxis）。血管異常增生（Angiodysplasia）呈典型雙軌（Tram-track）早期靜脈顯影，首選內視鏡治療。

**English:** Chronic mesenteric ischemia requires disease in at least two of the three main visceral arteries (celiac, SMA, IMA) due to extensive collateral networks, typically presenting with postprandial angina and managed with angioplasty and stenting. Diagnostic sensitivity for acute GI bleeding requires 0.5 to 1.0 mL/min for catheter DSA, 0.35 mL/min for CTA, and 0.2 to 0.4 mL/min for tagged red blood cell scintigraphy. Intra-arterial vasopressin infusion at 0.2 to 0.4 units/min effectively controls lower GI bleeding but is limited to a 24-hour duration due to tachyphylaxis and risks of systemic vasoconstrictive complications.

### 憩室出血核醫定位與超選擇性栓塞
![憩室出血核醫定位與超選擇性栓塞](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-721.png)

**中文：** 憩室出血（Diverticular bleed）為下消化道急性大出血的最常見原因。核醫標記紅血球掃描於 15、30、45 分鐘影像可清晰顯示肝彎處病灶累積與快速排至遠端結腸。選擇性 SMA 造影確認活性對比劑外溢後，必須使用微導管進行 <span style="color:#60a5fa">超選擇性微彈簧圈栓塞（Superselective microcoil embolization）</span>，精準封堵出血血管並最大程度降低腸缺血壞死風險。

**English:** Diverticular hemorrhage represents the most frequent etiology of severe, acute lower gastrointestinal bleeding in adults. Sequential tagged red blood cell scintigraphy at 15, 30, and 45 minutes localized abnormal radiotracer accumulation at the hepatic flexure with rapid distal colonic transit. Selective SMA angiography confirmed active contrast extravasation, which was successfully managed via superselective microcatheter coil embolization to achieve arterial hemostasis while preserving surrounding intestinal mucosal viability.

### 腎動脈狹窄、肌纖維發育不良與神經纖維瘤
![腎動脈狹窄、肌纖維發育不良與神經纖維瘤](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-722.png)

**中文：** 老年腎動脈狹窄主因粥狀硬化且累及開口處（Ostium），採氣球擴張與支架置放（ASTRAL 試驗顯示整體臨床效益存有爭議）。青年女性腎動脈狹窄主因 <span style="color:#60a5fa">肌纖維發育不良（FMD）</span>，累及腎動脈中遠段，雙側占 2/3；內膜中層發育不良（Medial fibroplasia, 占 80%）呈經典 <span style="color:#60a5fa">串珠狀（String of beads）</span>。FMD 首選 <span style="color:#60a5fa">單純氣球擴張術（PTA alone, 成功率 97%）</span>，<span style="color:#f87171">嚴禁例行置放支架</span>（以防內膜增生與影響日後擴張）。兒童腎動脈狹窄需考慮神經纖維瘤病（Neurofibromatosis）。

**English:** Renal artery stenosis in older adults is primarily atherosclerotic and ostial, whereas fibromuscular dysplasia (FMD) affects young women in the mid-to-distal renal artery and is bilateral in two-thirds of cases. The medial fibroplasia subtype accounts for 80% of FMD, displaying the classic "string of beads" angiographic appearance that responds exceptionally well to angioplasty alone with a 97% blood pressure benefit. Stenting is explicitly not recommended in renal FMD due to high rates of in-stent intimal hyperplasia and interference with future angioplasty retreatments.

### 腎細胞癌造影特徵與術前栓塞
![腎細胞癌造影特徵與術前栓塞](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-723.png)

**中文：** 腎細胞癌（RCC）在血管造影下呈現典型 <span style="color:#60a5fa">豐富血管性（Hypervascular）</span>、動靜脈分流（AV shunting）、靜脈湖（Venous lakes）與特徵性 <span style="color:#f87171">異常腫瘤新生血管（Bizarre neovascularity）</span>。對於大型或高血管性 RCC，外科切除前進行選擇性腎動脈栓塞，可顯著減少術中出血量並簡化手術切除過程。

**English:** Renal cell carcinomas typically demonstrate marked hypervascularity on selective renal angiography, characterized by extensive arteriovenous shunting, venous lakes, and bizarre neovascularity. Pre-operative transcatheter arterial embolization of hypervascular RCC tumors is performed to reduce intra-operative blood loss, decrease tumor bulk, and facilitate surgical resection. Accurate angiographic mapping ensures complete embolization of primary tumor supply while preserving normal surrounding renal parenchyma.

### 腎血管平滑肌脂肪瘤與腎臟外傷栓塞
![腎血管平滑肌脂肪瘤與腎臟外傷栓塞](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-724.png)

**中文：** 腎血管平滑肌脂肪瘤（AML）在造影上偶難與 RCC 鑑別，當 AML 直徑 > 4 cm 時發生自發性出血風險顯著增加。第四級腎臟外傷（Grade IV renal injury）造影顯示腎下極血流灌注缺失與 <span style="color:#f87171">活性對比劑外溢（Active extravasation）</span>。介入治療應以微導管進行 <span style="color:#60a5fa">超選擇性彈簧圈栓塞</span>，精準止血並保留最大量正常的腎實質功能。

**English:** Renal angiomyolipomas may mimic renal cell carcinoma on angiography due to hypervascularity and microaneurysms, with lesions larger than 4 cm carrying a high risk of spontaneous retroperitoneal hemorrhage. Grade IV renal trauma displays lower pole devascularization and active contrast extravasation on selective DSA. Transcatheter superselective coil embolization is the intervention of choice to arrest active hemorrhage while preserving maximal uninjured renal functional volume.

## 壓迫/門脈

### 正中弧狀韌帶症候群（MALS）
![正中弧狀韌帶症候群（MALS）](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-725.png)

**中文：** 正中弧狀韌帶症候群（MALS / Dunbar syndrome）係膈肌正中弧狀韌帶壓迫腹腔動脈幹開口所致。矢狀位 CT 或造影可見腹腔幹開口處呈 <span style="color:#60a5fa">特徵性鉤狀狹窄（Hook-like narrowing）</span>，且狹窄於 <span style="color:#60a5fa">呼氣末（Expiration）顯著加重</span>。臨床需注意多數輕微彎折為無症狀偶然發現，僅在伴隨餐後腹痛與體重減輕時行外科手術切斷韌帶解壓。

**English:** Median arcuate ligament syndrome results from extrinsic compression of the celiac artery origin by the fibrous median arcuate ligament of the diaphragm. Sagittal CT angiography demonstrates characteristic focal hook-like narrowing at the celiac origin, which worsens dynamically during full expiration. Clinical diagnosis requires exclusion of other causes for postprandial abdominal pain and weight loss, with definitive management consisting of surgical release of the median arcuate ligament.

### 腸系膜上動脈症候群（SMA Syndrome）
![腸系膜上動脈症候群（SMA Syndrome）](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-726.png)

**中文：** 腸系膜上動脈症候群（SMA syndrome，又稱 Wilkie syndrome）係十二指腸第三段受主動脈與 SMA 夾角壓迫所致。常見於體重快速減輕或後腹腔脂肪墊消失者。診斷標準為主動脈與 SMA 夾角 <span style="color:#60a5fa">< 25 度</span>（正常為 45–60 度），且主動脈-SMA 距離 <span style="color:#60a5fa">< 8 mm</span>（正常為 10–28 mm），造成十二指腸近端擴張與腸胃阻塞症狀。

**English:** Superior mesenteric artery syndrome, also known as Wilkie syndrome, involves acute compression of the third portion of the duodenum between the abdominal aorta and the SMA. The condition precipitates following rapid weight loss and loss of the protective retroperitoneal fat pad. Diagnostic CT criteria include an aortomesenteric angle of less than 25 degrees and an aortomesenteric distance reduced to less than 8 mm, leading to proximal duodenal obstruction.

### 門靜脈高壓與門脈壓力差（HVPG）測定
![門靜脈高壓與門脈壓力差（HVPG）測定](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-727.png)

**中文：** 門靜脈高壓評估金標準為測定 <span style="color:#60a5fa">肝靜脈壓力差（HVPG = Wedged HV pressure - Free HV pressure）</span>。正常 HVPG 為 <span style="color:#34d399">1–5 mmHg</span>；當 HVPG <span style="color:#60a5fa">> 5 mmHg</span> 時定義為門靜脈高壓；當 HVPG <span style="color:#60a5fa">≥ 10 mmHg</span> 時為具有臨床意義門脈高壓（形成靜脈曲張）；當 HVPG <span style="color:#f87171">≥ 12 mmHg</span> 時，食道靜脈曲張破裂大出血風險激增，為 TIPS 分流治療的主要適應症。

**English:** The gold standard for evaluating portal hypertension is measuring the hepatic venous pressure gradient (HVPG), calculated as wedged hepatic vein pressure minus free hepatic vein pressure. Normal HVPG ranges from 1 to 5 mmHg, with portal hypertension defined as an HVPG greater than 5 mmHg. Clinically significant portal hypertension with variceal formation occurs at an HVPG of 10 mmHg or greater, while an HVPG exceeding 12 mmHg confers a severe risk of lethal variceal hemorrhage.

### 經頸靜脈肝內門體分流術（TIPS）步驟
![經頸靜脈肝內門體分流術（TIPS）步驟](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-728.png)

**中文：** TIPS 手術步驟為：經右頸內靜脈置管至右肝靜脈、行 CO2 阻塞造影定位門靜脈，隨後穿刺肝實質進入門靜脈右支，以氣球擴張通道後置入 <span style="color:#60a5fa">PTFE 覆膜支架（Covered stent）</span>。TIPS 的治療目標為使分流後 HVPG 降至 <span style="color:#60a5fa">< 10–12 mmHg</span>。主要併發症包括 <span style="color:#f87171">肝性腦病變（Hepatic encephalopathy）</span> 與分流道急性血栓阻塞。

**English:** Transjugular intrahepatic portosystemic shunt creation involves right internal jugular vein access, right hepatic venography, wedged CO2 portography, and transhepatic needle puncture into the right portal vein. Following parenchymal tract balloon dilation, an ePTFE-covered stent graft is deployed to bridge the hepatic and portal systems. The hemodynamic goal of TIPS is reducing the post-procedure HVPG to below 10-12 mmHg, with major post-procedural risks including hepatic encephalopathy and shunt thrombosis.

### 下腔靜脈濾鏡放置適應症與位置
![下腔靜脈濾鏡放置適應症與位置](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-729.png)

**中文：** 下腔靜脈濾鏡（IVC Filter）適應症為下肢 DVT 患者存在抗凝血禁忌症、抗凝治療下仍復發肺栓塞（PE）或發生抗凝出血併發症。濾鏡釋放首選位置為 <span style="color:#22d3ee">最下方腎靜脈開口即刻下方</span>，以防異位腎靜脈成為血栓繞過濾鏡的管道。首選可回收型濾鏡，於血栓風險期過後應及時取出。

**English:** Inferior vena cava filter placement is indicated for patients with documented deep vein thrombosis who have absolute contraindications to anticoagulation, failure of anticoagulation with recurrent PE, or severe anticoagulation-related bleeding. The mandatory deployment location is immediately inferior to the lowest renal vein orifice to prevent thrombi from bypassing the filter via anomalous renal veins. Retrievable IVC filters are preferred and should be electively removed once the transient risk of pulmonary embolism resolves.

### 下腔靜脈解剖變異與濾鏡位置選擇
![下腔靜脈解剖變異與濾鏡位置選擇](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-730.png)

**中文：** 腎靜脈解剖變異包含 <span style="color:#22d3ee">環主動脈左腎靜脈（Circumaortic LRV, 2–10%）</span> 與 <span style="color:#22d3ee">主動脈後左腎靜脈（Retroaortic LRV, 2–7%）</span>。若存在此類變異，IVC 濾鏡必須放置於位置較低的變異腎靜脈開口下方。下腔靜脈變異包含雙下腔靜脈（Duplicated IVC, 0.5–3%）與巨下腔靜脈（Megacava, 直徑 > 28 mm），巨下腔靜脈需採雙側髂靜脈濾鏡放置。

**English:** Venous anatomical variations impacting IVC filter placement include circumaortic left renal vein (2% to 10%) and retroaortic left renal vein (2% to 7%), requiring filter placement inferior to the lowest anomalous vein orifice. IVC malformations include duplicated IVC (0.5% to 3%) and megacava defined as an IVC diameter exceeding 28 mm. Megacava invalidates standard IVC filter deployment, necessitating bilateral iliac vein filter placement or specialized megacava devices to prevent thromboembolism.

## 膽道與非血管

### 經皮穿肝膽道造影（PTC）與技術
![經皮穿肝膽道造影（PTC）與技術](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-731.png)

**中文：** 經皮穿肝膽道造影（PTC）適用於梗阻性黃疸。患者採仰臥位，先以 <span style="color:#60a5fa">22 Gauge 針</span> 經肋骨上緣穿刺肝實質（避開肋下神經血管束），注入對比劑使膽道顯影後，再以 <span style="color:#60a5fa">21 Gauge 針</span> 進行第二次低位標的膽管穿刺，隨後導入 0.018 吋導絲並換置 5–6 Fr 導管，置入內外膽道引流管（Internal-external biliary drain）。

**English:** Percutaneous transhepatic cholangiography begins with patient positioning in the supine stance and initial intrahepatic bile duct access using a 22-gauge needle inserted over the rib border to protect intercostal vessels. Following contrast injection and fluoroscopic biliary roadmap visualization, a second target puncture is performed as low as possible using a 21-gauge needle. A 0.018-inch wire is advanced into the biliary tree and exchanged for a 5 or 6 French coaxial catheter system to establish an internal-external biliary drainage catheter.

### 膽管損傷、硬化性膽管炎與惡性膽道梗阻
![膽管損傷、硬化性膽管炎與惡性膽道梗阻](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-732.png)

**中文：** 醫源性膽管損傷最常見於 <span style="color:#f87171">腹腔鏡膽囊切除術（Laparoscopic cholecystectomy）</span>，治療原則為置入膽道引流管進行膽汁轉流以利漏口癒合。原發性硬化性膽管炎（PSC）於造影下呈多發狹窄與擴張之 <span style="color:#60a5fa">串珠狀特徵（Beaded appearance）</span>。肝門部膽管癌（Klatskin tumor）造成之肝門梗阻，通常需要於右側與左側膽管各置入一支膽道引流管（雙管引流）。

**English:** Iatrogenic bile duct injury occurs most frequently during laparoscopic cholecystectomy, managed primarily via percutaneous biliary catheter insertion to provide bile diversion and permit ductal leak healing. Sclerosing cholangiography exhibits characteristic multifocal intrahepatic and extrahepatic biliary strictures producing a beaded appearance that increases the risk of cholangiocarcinoma. Malignant hilar biliary obstruction from a Klatskin tumor typically requires bilateral percutaneous biliary drains placed independently in the right and left hepatic ductal systems.

### 急性膽囊炎與經皮膽囊造廔引流術
![急性膽囊炎與經皮膽囊造廔引流術](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-733.png)

**中文：** 對於高手術風險之急性膽囊炎患者，<span style="color:#60a5fa">經皮膽囊造廔術（Percutaneous Cholecystostomy）</span> 為即時減壓的最佳處置。路徑首選 <span style="color:#22d3ee">經肝路徑（Transhepatic approach）</span>，利用肝實質包覆引流管路徑，可大幅降低膽汁洩漏引發 <span style="color:#f87171">膽汁性腹膜炎（Bile peritonitis）</span> 的風險；不論採經肝或經腹膜路徑，引流管均需留置至少 6 週，待纖維竇道成熟且複查膽道造影確認膽囊管與總膽管通暢後方可移除。

**English:** Percutaneous cholecystostomy provides critical gallbladder decompression in acalculous or calculous acute cholecystitis patients who are poor surgical candidates. The transhepatic approach is strongly preferred over the transperitoneal route because intervening liver parenchyma seals the tract, significantly reducing the risk of intraperitoneal bile leak and bile peritonitis. Regardless of transhepatic or transperitoneal access, the cholecystostomy tube must remain in place for at least six weeks; clinical improvement and repeat cholangiographic confirmation of cystic-duct and common-bile-duct patency are also required before removal.

### 經皮腎造廔術（PCN）與經皮胃造廔術
![經皮腎造廔術（PCN）與經皮胃造廔術](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-734.png)

**中文：** <span style="color:#60a5fa">經皮腎造廔術（PCN）</span> 適應症為阻塞性腎病，<span style="color:#f87171">腎積膿（Pyonephrosis）</span> 為急診適應症。患者採俯臥位，以 <span style="color:#60a5fa">22 Gauge 針</span> 經後外側穿刺 <span style="color:#22d3ee">後組腎盞（Posterior calyx）</span>，進針經過 Brödel 無血管區（腹側與背側腎動脈分支間之平面）以減少出血。經皮胃造廔術適應症為食道/頭頸/神經疾病灌食或腸道減壓；絕對禁忌症包括缺乏解剖視窗（如結腸位移）、嚴重胃靜脈曲張與無法矯正的凝血異常；固定採 3 個 T-fastener 胃固定扣，術後 24 小時評估無腹膜徵象即可使用，G-tube 需留置至少 1 個月以形成成熟竇道。

**English:** Percutaneous nephrostomy is indicated for urinary obstruction, with pyonephrosis representing an emergent indication requiring immediate decompression. Access is achieved in the prone position using a 22-gauge needle targeted posterolaterally toward a posterior calyx through Brödel's avascular zone between ventral and dorsal renal artery branches. Percutaneous gastrostomy requires insufflation, three T-fastener gastropexy clips, serial tract dilation, and absolute exclusion of uncorrectable coagulopathy or colonic interposition; the tube can be utilized after 24 hours and must remain in place for at least one month for tract maturation.

## 下肢

### 下肢動脈系統解剖結構
![下肢動脈系統解剖結構](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-735.png)

**中文：** 股總動脈（CFA）發自腹股溝韌帶下方，分支為深股動脈（PFA）與 <span style="color:#22d3ee">股淺動脈（SFA）</span>。SFA 穿過內收肌管（Hunter's canal）後改稱為膕動脈（Popliteal artery）。膕動脈於膝下發出 <span style="color:#22d3ee">脛前動脈（ATA）</span> 並延續為脛腓動脈幹，隨後分出 <span style="color:#22d3ee">脛後動脈（PTA）與腓動脈（Peroneal artery）</span> 灌注小腿與足部。

**English:** The common femoral artery originates inferior to the inguinal ligament, dividing into the profunda femoris and superficial femoral arteries. The superficial femoral artery traverses Hunter's adductor canal to become the popliteal artery in the popliteal fossa. Below the knee, the popliteal artery gives off the anterior tibial artery and continues as the tibioperoneal trunk, which subsequently bifurcates into the posterior tibial and peroneal (fibular) arteries supplying the lower leg and foot.

### 下肢靜脈解剖與 Leriche 症候群
![下肢靜脈解剖與 Leriche 症候群](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-736.png)

**中文：** 下肢靜脈解剖參見超音波章節。<span style="color:#60a5fa">Leriche 症候群（Leriche syndrome）</span> 係遠端腹主動脈與雙側髂動脈的慢性粥狀硬化完全阻塞。臨床典型三聯征包括：<span style="color:#f87171">臀腿部間歇性跛行（Claudication）</span>、<span style="color:#f87171">股動脈搏動消失</span> 與 <span style="color:#f87171">勃起功能障礙（Impotence）</span>。血管造影常顯示主動脈分叉處完全阻塞，依靠側支血管供血。

**English:** Lower extremity venous anatomy is comprehensively detailed in the ultrasound chapter. Leriche syndrome represents chronic atherosclerotic occlusion of the distal abdominal aorta at the iliac bifurcation, presenting with the classic clinical triad of buttock/thigh claudication, absent femoral pulses, and erectile dysfunction (impotence). Angiography demonstrates total cutoff of the infrarenal aorta with extensive compensatory collateral vessels repopulating distal lower extremity arteries.

### 髂動脈瘤特徵與修復適應症
![髂動脈瘤特徵與修復適應症](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-737.png)

**中文：** 髂動脈瘤（Iliac artery aneurysm）定義為直徑 <span style="color:#60a5fa">> 1.5 cm</span>。當直徑 <span style="color:#f87171">> 3.0 cm</span> 時破裂風險顯著增加，為腔內支架修復（EVAR / Iliac branch endoprosthesis）的治療適應症。髂動脈瘤多見於老年男性且常伴隨腹主動脈瘤（AAA），造影時需特別注意附壁血栓會使顯影管腔假性正常化。

**English:** An iliac artery aneurysm is defined as an iliac vessel diameter exceeding 1.5 cm, with surgical or endovascular repair strongly recommended once the diameter reaches 3.0 cm due to elevated rupture risk. Iliac aneurysms occur predominantly in elderly men and are frequently associated with abdominal aortic aneurysms. Angiography may underestimate true aneurysm size due to extensive mural thrombus, necessitating pre-procedural cross-sectional CT or MR imaging.

### 骨盆腔血管外傷與活性出血栓塞
![骨盆腔血管外傷與活性出血栓塞](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-738.png)

**中文：** 骨盆腔骨折（如恥骨聯合分離）極易拉扯剪裂內髂動脈分支引發大出血。X 光與 CT 造影常顯示骨盆腔大血腫及 <span style="color:#f87171">活性對比劑外溢（Active extravasation）</span>。急診介入應迅速行選擇性血管造影，使用微彈簧圈（Coils）或明膠海綿（Gelfoam）進行選擇性栓塞止血，以穩定血液動力學。

**English:** Severe pelvic trauma with pubic symphysis diastasis frequently lacerates branches of the internal iliac artery, producing massive retroperitoneal hematoma. Contrast-enhanced CT and pelvic DSA demonstrate foci of active contrast extravasation indicating ongoing arterial hemorrhage. Emergent transcatheter arterial embolization utilizing metallic coils or gelfoam slurry represents the essential intervention to arrest bleeding and stabilize patient hemodynamics.

### 子宮動脈栓塞術（UAE）與顆粒選擇
![子宮動脈栓塞術（UAE）與顆粒選擇](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-739.png)

**中文：** 子宮動脈栓塞術（UAE）的兩大主要適應症為症狀性子宮肌瘤與產後大出血（PPH），原文記載使用 <span style="color:#60a5fa">聚氯乙烯顆粒（Polyvinyl chloride particles）</span> 進行栓塞。治療目標為引發肌瘤出血性梗死，同時維持子宮內膜與肌層灌注以保留生育能力。嚴重併發症率約 1.25%，包括膿瘍、子宮內膜炎及卵巢壞死導致提早停經。

**English:** Uterine artery embolization is primarily indicated for symptomatic uterine leiomyomas and postpartum hemorrhage, utilizing polyvinyl chloride particles as documented in the original text. The therapeutic objective is selective hemorrhagic infarction of hypervascular fibroids while preserving sufficient baseline uterine perfusion to maintain future fertility. Serious complications occur in approximately 1.25% of cases, including pelvic abscess, endometritis, and non-target ovarian necrosis leading to premature menopause.

### 慢性下肢動脈阻塞與急性肢體缺血
![慢性下肢動脈阻塞與急性肢體缺血](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-740.png)

**中文：** 下肢周邊動脈疾病（PAD）依 Rutherford 分級評估（0 級無症狀，4 級靜止痛，5–6 級組織壞死）；<span style="color:#60a5fa">踝肱指數（ABI）< 0.9 為異常</span>，< 0.4 提示靜止痛。TASC-II 將股膕病變分級（Type A 首選腔內治療，Type D 首選手術）。急性肢體缺血呈 6P 症狀，經導管動脈內注射 <span style="color:#fb923c">tPA（0.5 mg/h，維持 48–72 小時）</span> 進行血栓溶解，治療期間需嚴密監測。

**English:** Peripheral arterial disease is categorized by Rutherford criteria and screened via Ankle-Brachial Index (ABI), where an ABI below 0.9 is abnormal and below 0.4 indicates severe rest pain. Femoropopliteal lesions follow TASC-II guidelines recommending endovascular therapy for Type A lesions and surgical bypass for Type D chronic total occlusions. Acute limb ischemia presents with the classic 6 Ps and is treated via catheter-directed tPA thrombolysis infused at 0.5 mg/h for 48 to 72 hours under strict ICU monitoring.

### 血栓溶解監測與膕動脈瘤處置
![血栓溶解監測與膕動脈瘤處置](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-741.png)

**中文：** 進行經動脈 tPA 血栓溶解時需於 ICU 監測：若血纖維蛋白原（Fibrinogen）降至 <span style="color:#f87171">< 150 mg/dL 應減慢 tPA 滴速</span>，若降至 <span style="color:#f87171">< 100 mg/dL 必須立即停藥</span>。膕動脈瘤（Popliteal aneurysm）為最常見周邊動脈瘤，<span style="color:#60a5fa">> 50% 為雙側性</span>，30–50% 伴隨腹主動脈瘤；當直徑 <span style="color:#f87171">> 2.0 cm</span> 時需行手術或腔內修復以防遠端栓塞。

**English:** Intra-arterial tPA thrombolysis requires strict ICU monitoring of hematocrit and fibrinogen levels; tPA infusion must be slowed if fibrinogen drops below 150 mg/dL and stopped immediately if fibrinogen decreases below 100 mg/dL. Popliteal artery aneurysms are the most common peripheral arterial aneurysms, over 50% are bilateral, and 30% to 50% associate with AAAs. Repair is strongly indicated once popliteal aneurysm diameter exceeds 2.0 cm to mitigate severe risks of distal thromboembolism.

## 上肢與文獻

### 上肢動靜脈血管解剖結構
![上肢動靜脈血管解剖結構](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-742.png)

**中文：** 上肢動脈主幹包含鎖骨下動脈、腋動脈（Axillary）與肱動脈，解剖變異包括 <span style="color:#60a5fa">橈動脈高位發源（High origin radial artery，約占 15%）</span>。淺層靜脈系統由外側頭靜脈（Cephalic vein）與內側貴要靜脈（Basilic vein）組成，匯入肱靜脈與腋靜脈，為血液透析血管通路（AV Fistula/Graft）建置的重要解剖基礎。

**English:** The primary arterial supply to the upper extremity comprises the subclavian, axillary, and brachial arteries, with a high origin of the radial artery occurring in approximately 15% of individuals. The superficial venous network consists of the lateral cephalic vein and medial basilic vein, which drain into the deep brachial and axillary veins. These superficial veins provide the anatomic foundation for creating autogenous hemodialysis arteriovenous fistulas and prosthetic grafts.

### 胸廓出口症候群（TOS）分類與解剖
![胸廓出口症候群（TOS）分類與解剖](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-743.png)

**中文：** 胸廓出口症候群（TOS）係神經血管束受壓所致。前/中斜角肌與第一肋骨圍成 <span style="color:#22d3ee">斜角肌三角（Interscalene triangle）</span>，內有臂神經叢與鎖骨下動脈通過；鎖骨下靜脈則位於前斜角肌前方。神經型 TOS 最常見；鎖骨下動脈受壓多由 <span style="color:#60a5fa">頸肋（Cervical rib，> 70%）</span> 或斜角肌肥大所致，症狀採 <span style="color:#60a5fa">Adson 測試</span> 評估（頭轉對側深吸氣致橈動脈脈搏減弱），首選解壓手術。

**English:** Thoracic outlet syndrome involves mechanical compression of the brachial plexus, subclavian artery, or subclavian vein at the thoracic outlet. The interscalene triangle is bounded by anterior and middle scalene muscles and the first rib, transmitting the subclavian artery and brachial plexus, while the subclavian vein runs anterior to the anterior scalene. Neurogenic TOS is the most common form; subclavian artery compression is associated with a cervical rib in over 70% of cases, evaluated via Adson's maneuver and treated with surgical decompression.

### Paget-Schroetter 症候群（鎖骨下靜脈血栓）
![Paget-Schroetter 症候群（鎖骨下靜脈血栓）](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-744.png)

**中文：** Paget-Schroetter 症候群（努力性血栓 Effort thrombosis）係年輕肌肉發達者因重複過頭運動致鎖骨下靜脈於肋鎖間隙受壓形成血栓。診斷性靜脈造影需評估手臂平放與外展雙側影像。治療首選 <span style="color:#60a5fa">經導管血栓溶解（Thrombolysis）</span>，隨後行 <span style="color:#f87171">第一肋骨切除術（1st rib resection）</span> 解除解剖壓迫；若無解剖壓迫方可採氣球擴張，嚴禁置放支架。

**English:** Paget-Schroetter syndrome represents effort thrombosis of the subclavian vein secondary to repetitive microtrauma at the costoclavicular space in athletic individuals. Diagnostic venography must evaluate both upper extremities in neutral and abducted positions to assess underlying mechanical narrowing and collateral development. Management requires catheter-directed thrombolysis followed by surgical first rib resection, with angioplasty reserved for non-mechanical stenoses and stenting generally avoided.

### 鎖骨下動脈竊血症候群與洗腎通路維護
![鎖骨下動脈竊血症候群與洗腎通路維護](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-745.png)

**中文：** <span style="color:#60a5fa">鎖骨下動脈竊血症候群（Subclavian Steal Syndrome）</span> 係鎖骨下動脈近端（椎動脈開口前）嚴重狹窄或阻塞，導致同側 <span style="color:#22d3ee">椎動脈血流倒流（Retrograde flow）</span> 供血予上肢。洗腎通路（AV Fistula/Graft）失能多由靜脈流出端狹窄所致，可採高壓氣球擴張（PTA）處置，診斷指標包含靜脈對肱動脈壓力比值 < 0.4。

**English:** Subclavian steal syndrome is caused by severe stenosis or occlusion of the subclavian artery proximal to the origin of the vertebral artery, resulting in retrograde blood flow down the ipsilateral vertebral artery to supply the arm. Hemodialysis access dysfunction commonly stems from venous outflow stenoses, which are managed via high-pressure or cutting balloon angioplasty when the venous-to-brachial artery pressure ratio drops below 0.4.

### 手部血管病變與小魚際錘症候群
![手部血管病變與小魚際錘症候群](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-746.png)

**中文：** <span style="color:#60a5fa">小魚際錘症候群（Hypothenar Hammer Syndrome）</span> 係掌心小魚際重複受到撞擊（如氣錘操作員或機械師），導致 <span style="color:#22d3ee">尺動脈於鉤骨鉤（Hook of hamate）處受損</span>，引發內膜損傷、血栓、動脈瘤或假性動脈瘤。造影顯示尺動脈段落性阻塞及第 4、5 指（或第 3 指）掌固有指動脈多發遠端栓塞，治療採外科手術修復。

**English:** Hypothenar hammer syndrome results from chronic repetitive trauma to the hypothenar eminence, injuring the ulnar artery as it crosses the hook of the hamate bone. This mechanical injury precipitates ulnar artery thrombosis, aneurysm, or pseudoaneurysm formation with downstream digital embolization into the fourth and fifth digits. Diagnostic angiography demonstrates segmental ulnar artery occlusion with multifocal distal digital artery occlusions, managed surgically via aneurysm resection and vascular reconstruction.

### 小魚際錘症候群影像與血管重建
![小魚際錘症候群影像與血管重建](/core_radiology/images/interventional/page-747.png)

**中文：** 小魚際錘症候群（Hypothenar Hammer Syndrome）的手動脈造影可清晰觀察到尺動脈於鉤骨鉤附近的節段性截斷，併發遠端末梢指動脈多發栓塞充盈缺損（常見於第 4 與第 5 指）。外科手術探索常可發現造影未顯影的尺動脈瘤體，切除病變段動脈瘤並進行血管移植重建，可有效恢復手部末梢血流灌注並緩解肢體缺血症狀。

Surgical exploration frequently reveals an ulnar artery aneurysm requiring resection and vascular bypass grafting to restore distal digital perfusion. Recognition of this clinical entity prevents inappropriate systemic therapy and directs definitive surgical intervention.

## 本章重點回顧 / Chapter Summary

1. **血管造影評估與穿刺安全 (Angiography & Access Safety)**
   - 血管造影判讀遵循 VIA 口訣（Vessels 血管性狀, Interventions 介入處置物, Anything else 異常發現），依序評估早期動脈相、晚期動脈相、實質相與靜脈相；若動脈相提前出現靜脈顯影提示動靜脈分流。
   - 股動脈穿刺首選股骨頭中下內側緣，針頭傾斜 45 度；高位穿刺（腹股溝韌帶上方）易引發致命的腹膜後血腫，低位穿刺易形成動靜脈廔管，直徑 > 1 cm 假性動脈瘤首選超音波導引凝血酶注射。
   - 空氣栓塞發生時應立即置於左側臥位並給予 100% 氧氣；PTA 氣球直徑應比血管大 10–20% 且需全程肝素抗凝；受外壓部位（如關節或壓迫症候群）應選用自擴式支架，支架直徑於動脈過大 10%、靜脈過大 20%。

2. **胸部與內臟血管介入 (Thoracic & Visceral Interventions)**
   - 肺動脈造影前需核對心電圖，若有左束支傳導阻滯（LBBB）需預先放置臨時起搏器，以防誘發完全性房室傳導阻滯。
   - 支氣管動脈栓塞術（BAE）必須嚴密排除脊髓前動脈（Artery of Adamkiewicz，呈現髮夾彎特徵），避免誤栓引發截癱。
   - 脾動脈瘤為最常見內臟動脈瘤，治療門檻為直徑 > 2.5 cm、有症狀或準備懷孕，採遠端再近端（Sandwich）彈簧圈栓塞；非阻塞性腸系膜缺血（NOMI）需直接動脈灌注 Papaverine 治療。

3. **腹盆腔、門脈與非血管處置 (Abdominopelvic, Portal & Non-vascular)**
   - 腎動脈肌纖維發育不良（FMD）好發於青年女性，呈經典串珠狀，首選單純氣球擴張術（PTA alone），嚴禁例行置放支架。
   - 門靜脈高壓評估金標準為肝靜脈壓力差（HVPG），當 HVPG ≥ 12 mmHg 時食道靜脈曲張大出血風險激增，為 TIPS PTFE 覆膜支架分流之主要適應症。
   - 經皮穿肝膽道造影（PTC）採 22G 針初刺、21G 針複刺置入內外引流管；經皮膽囊造廔術首選經肝路徑以防膽汁性腹膜炎；經皮腎造廔術（PCN）採 22G 針經 Brödel 無血管區進針；經皮胃造廔術採 3 個 T-fastener 固定，24 小時後評估無腹膜徵象即可使用，管路留置至少 1 個月以成熟竇道。

4. **周邊血管與上肢病變 (Peripheral & Upper Extremity Disease)**
   - 子宮動脈栓塞術（UAE）採用聚氯乙烯顆粒（Polyvinyl chloride particles）治療症狀性肌瘤與產後大出血；下肢 PAD Rutherford 評估中，ABI < 0.4 提示靜止痛；經導管 tPA 血栓溶解時需於 ICU 監測，Fibrinogen < 150 mg/dL 減慢滴速，< 100 mg/dL 必須停藥。
   - 胸廓出口症候群（TOS）動脈受壓多伴隨頸肋（> 70%）；Paget-Schroetter 症候群（鎖骨下靜脈努力性血栓）經血栓溶解後必須行第一肋骨切除術解壓；鎖骨下動脈竊血症候群係鎖骨下動脈近端阻塞致同側椎動脈血流倒流；小魚際錘症候群係尺動脈於鉤骨鉤處受損，需外科切除動脈瘤與血管重建。
