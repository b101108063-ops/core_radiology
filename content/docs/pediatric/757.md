Left aortic arch with aberrant right subclavian artery

•

A left aortic arch with an aberrant right subclavian artery is not a ring or a sling, and
is not a cause of stridor. It almost never causes symptoms, but in the rare case that it
does, dysphagia may result, which is called dysphagia lusoria.
